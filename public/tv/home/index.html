Yuma, AZ ⇄ Mexicali, B.C.

Establish your business in Yuma with WDF.

WDF's 3PL (third-party logistics) services start in Yuma, Arizona, in the U.S. Southwest, close to California. From there, we coordinate warehousing, inventory, order preparation and cross-border flows with our team in Mexicali, Baja California, Mexico, and connect your freight to the ports, carriers and crossings that link North America with supply chains worldwide.
